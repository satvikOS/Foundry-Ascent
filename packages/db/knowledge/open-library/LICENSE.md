# Open founder library: sources and licences

`open_library.json` holds short notes **in our own words** on openly reusable sources. No source's text is stored.
Every entry records its publisher, URL, licence, attribution, the section it comes from and the day it was read
(2026-11-24). Each licence below was read on the publisher's own page that day.

tax or investment advice, and say so where a qualified person has to decide.

**Looked at and left out.** OpenStax _Entrepreneurship_ and _Introduction to Business 2e_ (CC BY-NC-SA: not usable here);
OpenStax _Introduction to Business_, first edition (its pages name CC BY and, in the site notice, NC-SA terms and limits on
